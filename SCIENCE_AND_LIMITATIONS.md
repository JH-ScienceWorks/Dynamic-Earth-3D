# Dynamic Earth 3D v2.0E — science and limitations / mallit ja rajoitukset

Opetuskäyttöön / For education. All ten studies remain available: Maa / Earth; Taivas / Sky; Todellinen mittakaava / True scale; Kuun vaiheet / Moon phases; Kuun pinta / Moon surface; Pimennykset / Eclipses; Maan sisärakenne / Earth interior; Laattatektoniikka / Plate tectonics; Magneettikenttä ja aurinkotuuli / Magnetic field and solar wind; Aurinko / Sun.

- Earth/Sun astronomy uses the retained approximate solar coordinates and sidereal rotation model. Earth is a sphere of mean radius 6,371 km. Educational view compresses separations/enlarges bodies; True scale uses one physical length factor for sphere radii and centre separations. Camera distance, screen scale bar and centre-to-centre separation are distinct quantities.
- The ordinary Moon view uses a uniform circular orbit (27.3217 days, 5.145° inclination), simplified synchronous orientation, no precise lunar ephemeris/libration. Its centre distance is 384,400 km in that model; Sun distance changes with the approximate solar model. Displayed centre distances are calculated/documented model values.
- Rataopaste on havainnollistava projektio. Se ei kuvaa radan tarkkaa kolmiulotteista suuntaa tai muotoa.
- The orbit guide is a schematic projection. It does not depict the orbit's exact three-dimensional orientation or shape.
- Eclipse Lab is separate: retained Astronomy Engine 2.1.19, five curated validated demonstrations and bounded observer/shadow calculations. It does not turn the ordinary Moon or scale scene into a precision eclipse model. It is not an observing/safety/navigation forecast. Never look at the Sun without appropriate solar-observation protection.
- Seasons overview uses four illustrative Earth positions, parallel axes and approximate dates. Enlarged close-up and oblique orbit diagram do not share one scale. Seasons primarily result from axial tilt, solar altitude and day length. Temperature/weather are not calculated.
- Interior: ideal four-region sphere; 30 km crust, boundaries at depths 2,900/5,150 km. Mostly solid mantle, liquid outer core, solid inner core. Colors and cutaways are teaching aids, not photographs; enlarged-crust mode explicitly changes visual proportions. No PREM, regional crust, convection or thermal model.
- Tectonics: PB2002 historical plate-boundary database, not real-time plate positions. Mechanism diagrams are qualitative; motions/time arrows are illustrative. Continents are not plates and crust is not the whole lithosphere.
- Magnetism: fixed educational dipole tilt 11°; illustrative meridian, no WMM/IGRF coefficients, epoch or navigation accuracy. Solar-wind boundary uses the documented Shue et al. 1998 empirical magnetopause model within its assumptions; not a space-weather forecast.
- Sun: idealized six-layer model; rounded representative values, greatly enhanced atmosphere/surface patterns, qualitative granulation and example sunspots. Procedural shading is not a solar observation or simulation of plasma dynamics.
- Earth day/cloud/night and Moon maps are historical mosaics. Clouds/night lights are not current weather. Normal maps only shade spherical surfaces. Guides/labels may clip or hide at extreme camera framing.
